# Manex AI

Manex AI (Manex AI GmbH) builds autonomous enterprise AI for industrial and
manufacturing organizations. Made in Europe, built to operate under the
strictest compliance standards for AI in critical industry.

## What we do

We deliver one production system for autonomous manufacturing. It unifies the
data, decisions, and knowledge of an enterprise so AI agents, workflows, and
applications can act on full context instead of fragments.

## Products

- Kosmos — the Autonomous Enterprise AI Platform; models the domains a
  business runs on and lets agents act on them. The foundation for all suites.
- Horizon — the distribution platform that makes a solution built at one site
  instantly available to every other, so local innovation compounds.

## Suites (built on Kosmos)

- Blackbelt — Autonomous CAQ (computer-aided quality) system.
- Takt — Autonomous MES (manufacturing execution system).
- Gemba — Autonomous shopfloor system.

## Target market

Industrial and manufacturing OEMs and their suppliers, including aerospace,
automotive, chemistry, electronics, semiconductors, pharma & biotech, heavy
equipment, industrial machinery, and home appliances.

## Trust

Cloud, on-premise, or any environment. Data residency by the customer's
standards; GDPR compliance available for European customers. Certified to
ISO 27001 and TISAX. Enterprise access control and full audit history.
